## Description

Shinkokyu is an app for breathing deeply, as if you were standing in the middle of nature.

When you're worn out, stressed, or your focus is gone. On restless nights, too.
Open the app and tap "Start" — a forest fills the screen, and the sound of a stream,
birds, and wind quietly gathers around you.

When the circle swells, breathe in through your nose.
When it shrinks, breathe out slowly through your mouth.
In for 4 seconds, out for 8. Just repeat it for 3 minutes.

After 3 minutes, a soft bell rings and it quietly ends.
It never cuts off mid-breath — it waits until you've breathed all the way out.

——

◯ Lose yourself in nature
Photos of forests, waterfalls, and lakes from across Japan, with the sounds recorded there.
The screen and sound alone open up the outdoors in front of you. The view drifts to a new
place every 30 seconds, slowly zooming, layered with water, birdsong, and night insects.
As your attention settles on the nature in front of you, the noise in your head loosens,
and you slip into three minutes of simply breathing.

◯ Nothing to choose
No courses, no background music to pick. Just open and tap. So you don't waste time
deciding, the app chooses the scenery for you.

◯ Asks nothing of you
No account, no notifications, no goals. If you can't keep it up, nothing blames you.

◯ No connection, no records
We collect nothing about you, and never go online. The only thing kept is "how many times
you opened it this week" — quietly visible in the home-screen widget.

——

・A slow rhythm: in for 4 seconds, out for 8
・Photos of forests, waterfalls, and lakes across Japan, with their ambient sound
・The screen turns quiet at night
・Keep going with the screen off — sound alone is enough
・Plays even with the silent switch on (earphones recommended)

As first aid for the hard moments.
When you want to let your shoulders drop, here it is.

## App Review Information — Notes (English)

> During a 3-minute breathing session this app plays ambient nature sound (forest, stream,
> birdsong). It uses UIBackgroundModes: audio so the ambience and the closing bell keep
> playing while the screen is locked. That is the only use of background audio. The app
> collects and transmits no user data, and has no account or login.
>
> Photos are used under the Unsplash License, ambient sounds under CC0 (freesound),
> and fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
